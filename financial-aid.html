By taking advantage of our amazing scholarships and financial aid options, you’ll pay way less and, in some cases, even nothing. Options for paying for your education?
We’ve got them.

Grants

Grants do not need to be repaid! These are need-based funds provided through the U.S Department of Education. Includes the Federal Pell Grant program.

Work-study

Get real world job experience while you work, study, and earn money for educational expenses.

Direct Loans

A last resort in most cases at LSSC, but student loans can be used to cover tuition and fees, books and supplies, housing and food.

Federal Aid Starts with FAFSA

There’s only one way to apply for federal student aid, and that’s with the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). The amount of financial aid you receive is based on the information you provide on your FAFSA. So, take the time now to fill it out! Need assistance? We’re here.

You’ll need this! The Lake-Sumter State College FAFSA school code is 001502.

Grants

As a Sunshine State resident, you may be eligible for the Florida Student Assistance Grant (FSAG) or the First Generation Matching Grant (FGMG).

Scholarships

You may be eligible for one, or more, scholarships funded by the state of Florida and administered by LSSC.

Florida Bright Futures

All that hard work in high school is about to pay off. The Florida Bright Futures Scholarship rewards students for their academic achievements.

Institutional Scholarships

We got you! Institutional scholarships are available to all students who meet the general eligibility criteria. Scholarships include need-based, talent, and service.

Foundation

Thanks to generous LSSC supporters, we’re able to award over $500,000 annually to students. Do you have a 2.0 GPA or above? Then you should definitely apply!

Outside Scholarships

The money is out there! Let us help you get connected to some amazing scholarship opportunities, including those for veterans, first-gen students, and international students.
